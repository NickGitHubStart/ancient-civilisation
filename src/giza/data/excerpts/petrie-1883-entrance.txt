Having, then, fixed the original position of the doorway of the Pyramid, we may state that it was at 668.2 ± .1 above the pavement of the Pyramid; 524.1 ± .3 horizontally inside (or S. of) the N. edge of the Pyramid casing; and its middle 287.0 ± .8 E. of the centre of the Pyramid.
